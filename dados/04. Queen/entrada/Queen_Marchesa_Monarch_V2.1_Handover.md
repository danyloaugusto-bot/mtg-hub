**Framework:** Handover Master 2.0 + teoria 8×8.

## 1. Identidade operacional
Queen Marchesa é um deck Mardu Monarch/Politics que começa discreto, desenvolve ramp e Pillow Fort, estabelece a monarquia no mid game e transforma a compra recorrente de cartas em controle político, remoção seletiva e pressão progressiva. No late game, Courts, fichas e finalizadores convertem essa vantagem em vitória. Preservar as seis Courts, cartas premium e a personalidade política do deck.

## 2. Plano de jogo
- **Early:** desenvolver mana e defesa com baixo perfil; não descarregar todas as permanentes.
- **Middle:** baixar Marchesa em uma janela segura, adquirir a monarquia e gerar valor das Courts e draw redundante.
- **End:** fechar com Approach of the Second Sun, token pressure, Forth Eorlingas!, Caesar, Assemble the Legion, Skyline Despot ou Ruinous Ultimatum seguido de pressão.
- **Política:** a monarquia é um recurso, não uma obrigação a qualquer custo. Às vezes vale deixar outro jogador pegar a coroa se isso redirecionar ataques ou consumir recursos adversários.

## 3. Reconciliação numérica
- **100 cartas:** 1 commander + 99 maindeck.
- **8×8:** 35 land slots + 64 spells em oito grupos de oito.
- **Land package:** 32 terrenos físicos + 3 MDFCs = 35 cartas alocadas ao pacote de terrenos.
- **Maze of Ith** é terreno físico, mas não produz mana.
- **MDFCs:** Bloodsoaked Insight // Sanguine Morass; Glasswing Grace // Age-Graced Chapel; Legion Leadership // Legion Stronghold. Neste modelo 8×8, são alocadas ao pacote de terrenos; a face de spell continua funcional, sem dupla contagem nos 64 spells.

## 8. Watchlist
1. **Approach of the Second Sun:** can the deck see and cast it a second time at a realistic pace?
2. **Sphere of Safety:** is enchantment density high enough to tax attacks materially?
3. **Descent into Avernus:** is the acceleration worth the threat it creates?
4. **Forth Eorlingas! / Caesar / Assemble the Legion / Skyline Despot:** which finisher closes games most reliably?
5. **Color fixing:** how often is one color missing on turns 2–4?
6. **Monarch retention:** track turns holding the crown, life lost to takebacks and cards drawn.
7. **Inkshield:** was it live at the right moment, or dead in hand?

## 9. Playtest plan
For each game record pod strength, opening hand, mulligan, turn Marchesa entered, Monarch turns retained, cards drawn from Monarch, cards drawn from engines, number/timing of wipes, Inkshield impact, MVP/dead cards, win/loss, and which political decision mattered.

Play 5–8 games before more cuts. Look for mana screw/color imbalance, ramp drawn too late, hands full of expensive defense but no value engine, too many resets, and whether the deck can transition from Pillow Fort into an actual win.

## 10. Rules and source notes
- Queen Marchesa enters and makes you Monarch.
- If an opponent is Monarch at your upkeep, Marchesa creates a 1/1 Assassin with deathtouch and haste.
- The Monarch draws a card at the beginning of their end step; combat damage to the Monarch changes the crown.
- MDFCs are counted consistently in this 8×8 model: land bucket only, spell faces remain castable but are not double-counted in the 64.
- Maze of Ith is a land slot, not a mana source.
- Inkshield is defense and token-based victory conversion.
- Approach of the Second Sun requires the second cast to win.
