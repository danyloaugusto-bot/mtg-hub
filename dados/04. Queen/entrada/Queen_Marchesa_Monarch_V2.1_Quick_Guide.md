# Queen Marchesa Monarch — Quick Play Guide (V2.1)

## Early (T1–3)
Develop colored mana, ramp and one discrete layer of protection. Do not unload every tax piece before the deck has a value engine. Prefer at least two lands, a reliable early play and access to relevant colors by turn four.

## Middle (T4–7)
Play Queen Marchesa when you can capitalize on Monarch draw. Choose Courts by board state: Court of Grace for board presence, Court of Locthwain for resource pressure, Court of Ire for damage, Court of Ambition for attrition, Court of Ardenvale for recursion, and Court of Embereth for combat scaling. Preserve an answer for the player most likely to take the crown or snowball.

## End (T8+)
Pick a closing lane:
- Approach of the Second Sun supported by draw and time.
- Token pressure from Assemble the Legion, Caesar, Forth Eorlingas!, Ophiomancer and Skyline Despot.
- Ruinous Ultimatum followed by immediate pressure.
- Inkshield after a large committed attack, potentially creating a lethal counterattack.

## Politics
Offer benefits to opponents who attack the Monarch or archenemy. Use Disrupt Decorum to redirect the table. Do not spend removal merely because a threat exists; answer threats that compromise life total, card engines or the ability to close.

## Record each playtest
Opening hand, mulligan, turn Marchesa entered, Monarch turns held, cards drawn, colors missed, wipe timing, Inkshield impact, finisher used, win/loss, MVP and dead cards.
