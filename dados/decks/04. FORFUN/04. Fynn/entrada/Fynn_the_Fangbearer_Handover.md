# HANDOVER ESPECÍFICO — FYNN, THE FANGBEARER
**Versão:** V2 proposta | **Verificação:** 2026-10-09 | **Status:** PROJEÇÃO — NÃO VALIDADA POR PLAYTEST

## 1. Identity
- **Comandante:** Fynn, the Fangbearer
- **Identidade:** Mono-Green (verde + cartas/terrenos incolores permitidos)
- **Estratégia:** Aggro de veneno, com alta densidade de criaturas baratas com deathtouch, evasão/trample, proteção na janela decisiva e proliferate como acelerador/fechamento.
- **Frase de construção:** Mono-Green Fynn Poison Aggro — colocar ameaças baratas com toque mortífero cedo, conectar dano de combate para produzir counters de veneno e usar proliferate para fechar partidas após estabelecer veneno nos oponentes.

## 2. Commander
Fynn custa {1}{G}, é 1/3 com deathtouch. A habilidade não concede infect: ela recompensa criaturas suas com deathtouch que causem dano de combate a um jogador, colocando dois poison counters nesse jogador. Em condições ideais, cinco conexões desse tipo eliminam um adversário que começa sem veneno, então conectar é mais relevante do que aumentar power sem infect.

**Regra verificada:** dano de infect continua sendo dano; habilidades que disparam quando uma criatura causa dano funcionam normalmente. Uma criatura com infect e deathtouch que conecta pode gerar poison counters pelo dano de infect e ainda disparar Fynn para mais dois. (Wizards, Scars of Mirrodin Mechanics)

## 3. Strategy & Win Conditions
### Primária
- Ataques repetidos com criaturas de 1–2 manas com deathtouch; cada conexão gera dois poison counters via Fynn.
- Aumentar a densidade de criaturas baratas que conseguem atacar e a probabilidade de conectar.
### Secundária
- Infect/Toxic como pacote complementar para pressionar o contador de veneno; distinguir as palavras-chave: Toxic não é Infect.
- Proliferate com Bloated Contaminator, Adaptive Sporesinger, Pollenbright Druid, Expand the Sphere, Glistening Sphere, Cankerbloom, Karn's Bastion e V2 com Evolution Sage/Contagion Clasp.
### Fechamento
- Triumph of the Hordes: finisher proposto de quatro manas.
- Proliferate quando adversários já têm veneno.
- Ataque protegido com multiple cheap deathtouch bodies e evasion/trample.
### Fraqueza estrutural
- Remoção/wipes que interrompem o ataque decisivo; bloqueadores e pillow fort; falta de gás após limpar a mão; ameaças pontuais muito caras que não ajudam a conectar.

## 5. Current diagnosis
- **Current score estimado:** 5.9/10 para a proposta declarada; não é pontuação objetiva nem testada.
- **Pontos fortes:** comandante de custo 2; diversas criaturas deathtouch de baixo custo; boas cartas multifuncionais como Cankerbloom, Expand the Sphere, Plague Myr, Bloated Contaminator, Flesh Burrower e Insidious Fungus; proteção pontual já presente.

- **Infect vs. Toxic:** Plague Myr tem Infect. Bloated Contaminator tem Toxic 1 e proliferate no dano de combate; Branchblight Stalker tem Toxic 2, mas nenhum dos dois tem deathtouch por si só e, portanto, não gera o trigger do Fynn sem ganhar deathtouch. (Wizards mechanics + MTG.WTF Oracle cross-check)
- **Mana base:** 35 lands está dentro da referência do framework. Temple of the False God é uma fonte arriscada no early; Throne of the High City é uma linha de Monarch que não ajuda diretamente o plano de veneno.

## 10. Cuts and upgrades — V2
### Nonland swaps (18)
- **OUT Blackblade Reforged → IN Rancor** [B] — Replace large power-scaling equipment with 1-mana recurring trample; helps deathtouch connect and keeps the curve low.
- **OUT Caged Sun → IN Toski, Bearer of Secrets** [A] — A 6-mana mana doubler is slow for aggro; Toski creates repeatable card advantage from successful attacks.
- **OUT Colossification → IN Triumph of the Hordes** [S] — Replace a 7-mana aura that taps its target with a 4-mana overrun/infect finisher that can convert a board into lethal poison.
- **OUT Contagion Engine → IN Contagion Clasp** [A] — Keep proliferate support, but lower the entry cost from six mana to two; the activation remains a late-game mana sink.
- **OUT Courage in Crisis → IN Evolution Sage** [S] — Replace a one-shot 3-mana proliferate effect with repeatable landfall proliferate, naturally supported by the land base and ramp.
- **OUT End-Raze Forerunners → IN Ohran Frostfang** [A] — Frostfang grants team-wide deathtouch and draws cards on combat damage, advancing both Fynn triggers and the gas problem.
- **OUT Ezuri's Predation → IN Heroic Intervention** [A] — Eight mana for a reactive board effect is slow; global protection preserves the attack/poison board against wipes and targeted interaction.
- **OUT Font of Mythos → IN Sylvan Library** [S] — Group draw gives opponents the same advantage; Library improves your own selection and helps find early creatures, protection and finishers.
- **OUT Genesis Wave → IN Llanowar Elves** [A] — Trade a slow, highly mana-dependent top end for turn-one acceleration that can enable Fynn plus another one-drop on turn two.
- **OUT Loxodon Warhammer → IN Wasteland Viper** [A] — Wasteland Viper is a one-mana deathtouch body and can act as a combat trick from hand; better early pressure and role compression.
- **OUT Mask of Avacyn → IN Tyvar's Stand** [A] — One-mana protection that scales into a pump spell is more flexible and cheaper than casting/equipping the Mask.
- **OUT Preposterous Proportions → IN Infectious Bite** [S] — Convert a 6-mana generic pump/draw effect into cheap creature removal that also seeds poison on each opponent.
- **OUT Quietus Spike → IN Narnam Renegade** [A] — The equipment's life-halving trigger does not advance poison; Renegade supplies another one-mana deathtouch attacker.
- **OUT Scytheclaw → IN Moss Viper** [A] — Five-mana equipment focused on life loss becomes a one-mana deathtouch/reach attacker for Fynn's primary trigger.
- **OUT Silklash Spider → IN Gnarlwood Dryad** [B] — The five-mana anti-flyer sweeper is meta-dependent; a one-mana deathtouch body improves early pressure and board density.
- **OUT Skyreaping → IN Venerated Rotpriest** [A] — Replace conditional anti-flyer damage with a cheap poison engine that rewards targeting your creatures with protection/pump spells.
- **OUT Windstorm → IN Trailblazer's Boots** [B] — Trade the narrow anti-flyer slot for repeatable evasion, so deathtouch attackers can reach players more reliably.
- **OUT Soul's Majesty → IN Nature's Lore** [S] — Replace a sorcery draw spell dependent on a large body with efficient two-mana Forest ramp; repeatable draw is upgraded separately by Toski and Ohran Frostfang.

### Mana-base swaps (2; physical land count stays 35)
- **OUT Temple of the False God → IN Forest** [S] — Avoid a land that can fail to produce mana in early turns; a basic Forest is always online.
- **OUT Throne of the High City → IN Inkmoth Nexus** [A] — Monarchy is a side quest; Inkmoth supplies evasive infect pressure and can become a poison carrier.

## 12. Watchlist
- Windstorm / Silklash Spider: re-evaluate if flyers dominate the local meta.
- Hornet Queen: seven-mana top-end, but four flying deathtouch tokens can provide multiple Fynn triggers; test whether it sits dead in hand.
- Karametra's Acolyte: can generate a lot of green mana, but it is a four-mana setup creature; track whether it accelerates a relevant turn.
- Disorienting Choice: opponents decide whether to exile selected artifacts/enchantments; measure whether the land-ramp mode actually happens.
- Branchblight Stalker: Toxic 2 contributes poison, but does not trigger Fynn unless it has deathtouch.
- Glistening Sphere: test whether the 3-mana ramp + proliferate is timely enough.
- Trailblazer's Boots: test actual evasion across the pod; swap back to the best cheap deathtouch threat if it fails to connect.
- Proliferate package: only advances players already carrying poison counters. Spread initial poison first.

## 13. Rules & interactions
- A player with ten or more poison counters loses the game.
- Infect damage to players is damage replaced by poison counters; damage-trigger abilities still work normally.
- Toxic adds poison in addition to normal damage/life loss and is not synonymous with Infect.
- Proliferate adds a counter only to a chosen player/permanent that already has a counter.
- Fynn's trigger does not require Fynn to remain on the battlefield after the trigger is on the stack / after lethal poison counters are given, according to the official ruling.
- Triumph of the Hordes is projected as the primary "close now" spell; protect the attack turn when possible.

## 14. Playtest plan
Record 5–10 games before re-rating:
1. Turn Fynn enters; first poison counter and first lethal threat.
2. Poison per combat, number of deathtouch bodies that connect, and whether evasion is enough.
3. Hand size on turns 4 and 6; identify which draw engine actually replenishes gas.
4. Whether the protection package saves a lethal attack from interaction or wipes.
5. Whether proliferate is active (opponents already poisoned), and whether its activation costs tempo.
6. Cards stranded at 5+ MV; note especially Hornet Queen, Return of the Wildspeaker, and Ohran Frostfang.
7. Do not label a score "Tested" until real games have been logged.

## 16. Decisions that must not be lost
- Preserve mono-green identity.
- Strategy priority is Aggro / kill quickly; do not turn this into green goodstuff or a big-mana value pile.
- Fynn triggers on deathtouch combat damage to players; poison counters from this trigger are fixed at two regardless of power.
- Toxic and Infect are different; distinguish them when analysing cards.
- Proliferate is a closer/accelerator after poison is seeded, not a replacement for initial combat damage.
- Preserve 35 land slots in the first V2 and test before cutting more lands.
- Current 5.9 and projected 8.6 are assessments; no playtests have been supplied.
