## 2. Strategic thesis
**Early:** deploy efficient Humans and low-MV infrastructure, while preserving card advance.
**Mid:** cast Greymond when the board already contains multiple Humans; the commander's 4-Human threshold should turn the existing board into a meaningful clock.
**Late:** protect the board, deny the decisive response window, and convert the wide board into lethal pressure in one or two attack cycles.

The north star is not “the best mono-white cards.” It is:
> **the best Humans that also advance pressure, tokens, draw, tax, removal, protection or combat.**

## 4. Score
Current list: **8.4/10**
V2 projected: **9.4/10**
10/10: **reserved for a playtested configuration**, not merely a theoretical list.

The 9.4 projection is supported by:
- stronger Human quality and density;
- doubling the low-cost Human count;
- replacing defensive pillow-fort pieces with proactive Human taxes;
- adding premium Human token/scaling engines;
- improving one-mana interaction;
- preserving the strong protection and draw shell.

## 5. V2 — 13 OUT
- **Storm Herd** — 8-mana Pegasus swarm; powerful standalone but too late and not Human.
- **Temple of the False God** — Conditional mana and no tribal payoff; poor early draw.
- **Nyx Lotus** — High ceiling but five-mana setup is too slow for aggressive early turns.
- **Magus of the Disk** — Human wipe on a body, but slow and symmetrical.
- **Caduceus, Staff of Hermes** — High ceiling protection/buff, but slow/conditional equipment slot.
- **Ghostly Prison** — Defensive tax; V2 replaces it with proactive Human taxes.
- **Windborn Muse** — Strong pillow-fort effect, but non-Human and redundant with Prison/Baird.
- **Court of Ardenvale** — Four-mana monarch/recursion is slow for this pressure plan.
- **Hour of Revelation** — Powerful but redundant late sweeper; one less reset improves go-wide consistency.
- **Duelist's Heritage** — Very strong combat card, but non-Human and narrower than the Human alternatives.
- **Baird, Steward of Argive** — Good Human tax body; cut only because premium Human disruption is better.
- **Masako the Humorless** — Useful combat surprise, but too narrow for a precious Human slot.
- **Michiko Konda, Truth Seeker** — Reactive deterrent; lower ceiling than proactive Human hatebears.

## 6. V2 — 13 IN
- **Adeline, Resplendent Cathar** — Best-in-class Human token pressure: every attack creates a Human for each opponent you attack.
- **Thalia's Lieutenant** — Premium two-mana Human scaling engine: ETB counters on the team; later Humans grow the Lieutenant.
- **Champion of the Parish** — Best-in-class one-drop for this commander: every subsequent Human permanently grows it.
- **Coppercoat Vanguard** — Human lord with protection against noncombat damage; excellent for wide Human boards.
- **Boromir, Warden of the Tower** — Human + vigilance + free-spell check + team indestructible sacrifice.
- **Grand Abolisher** — Human hatebear that protects the decisive turn by shutting off opposing spells/activated abilities.
- **Drannith Magistrate** — Human hatebear that blocks casting from non-hand zones; strong against commanders, graveyards and cascade-like effects.
- **Knight of the White Orchid** — Early Human first striker that can fetch a Plains when behind; improves tempo without leaving tribal plan.
- **Mentor of the Meek** — Human draw engine; small Humans/tokens become cards.
- **Myrel, Shield of Argive** — Human token engine and turn-lock; Soldiers are not Humans, but the engine is elite.
- **Odric, Master Tactician** — Human combat closer; a wide attack can let you dictate legal blocks.
- **Thalia, Guardian of Thraben** — Efficient two-mana Human tax piece; attacks while taxing noncreature spells.
- **Swords to Plowshares** — One of the highest-quality one-mana creature removals; a necessary non-Human exception.

### Thalia's Lieutenant

### Champion of the Parish
A true turn-one Human payoff: every later Human makes it permanently larger. This is exactly what the early-game plan was missing.

### Coppercoat Vanguard
Human lord plus resilience for a wide Human board. The card increases the payoff of maintaining Human density.

### Boromir, Warden of the Tower
Human body plus a free-spell check and a sacrifice mode that gives the team indestructible. citeturn625635search1

### Grand Abolisher
A premium Human for protecting the decisive turn. It is especially valuable when the deck has enough pressure to win immediately.

### Drannith Magistrate
Proactive Human hatebear for commanders, graveyard/exile casting and other non-hand casting patterns.

### Knight of the White Orchid
Human + first strike + conditional Plains acceleration. This preserves the tribal plan while improving early tempo.

### Mentor of the Meek
Human card-advantage engine that becomes more attractive as the deck adds cheap Humans and tokens.

### Myrel, Shield of Argive
Human token engine and a powerful “your turn” interaction lock. Important nuance: Myrel's tokens are **colorless Soldiers, not Humans**, so it is included because of its engine/protection power, not because it directly receives Greymond's Human buff. citeturn625635search0

### Odric, Master Tactician
Human combat closer. When Odric and at least three other creatures attack, you choose how the defending creatures block, within legal restrictions. citeturn625635search11

### Thalia, Guardian of Thraben
Efficient two-mana Human tax. It shifts the interaction burden toward opponents without making the deck passive.

### Swords to Plowshares
The deliberate non-Human exception. The objective is not “100% Humans”; the objective is an optimized Human deck. Premium one-mana removal is worth a slot.

## 8. What was removed and why
The cuts were selected primarily from four patterns:
1. **Slow:** Storm Herd, Nyx Lotus, Court of Ardenvale.
2. **Conditional/low tempo:** Temple of the False God.
3. **Defensive redundancy:** Ghostly Prison, Windborn Muse.
4. **Narrow/redundant or weaker than premium Human replacements:** Magus of the Disk, Baird, Masako, Michiko, Duelist's Heritage, Hour of Revelation, Caduceus.

This does not mean these are “bad cards.” It means their opportunity cost is too high when the stated target is a top-tier Human tribal shell.

## 9. What stays deliberately non-Human
The deck should not force tribal purity at the expense of function.

Intentional exceptions include:
- Sol Ring
- Smothering Tithe
- Skullclamp
- Teferi's Protection
- Flawless Maneuver
- Metallic Mimic
- Enduring Innocence
- Selfless Spirit
- selected premium removal

The rule is:
> **A non-Human must justify the slot by doing something a Human alternative cannot reasonably replace.**

## 11. Key synergies

### Greymond + 4 Humans
Do not treat Greymond as “a 4-mana creature.” The deployment target is a board where the Human threshold makes the commander immediately relevant.

### Adeline + Human scaling
Adeline creates Humans on attack, which then feed Champion, Lieutenant, Mimic, Horn of Gondor and Greymond.

### Metallic Mimic + Humans
Name Human. Later Humans enter with a +1/+1 counter. Mimic itself is a Shapeshifter, so this is an intentional exception to tribal purity.

### Mirror Entity
Changeling means it is a Human. Its activated ability turns a wide board into a lethal power spike.

### Horn of Gondor
The larger the Human army, the more bodies the Horn can generate, making it a self-reinforcing engine.

### The Wandering Rescuer
The deck often becomes vulnerable after attacking because its creatures are tapped. The Rescuer's hexproof clause is particularly strong in that exact state.

### Your Temple Is Under Attack
Perfect example of group 8: indestructibility when the table is about to wipe, or political draw when protection is not needed.

## 12. 10/10 roadmap
A theoretical list should not be called 10/10.

For the next iteration, record for **20–30 games**:
- opening-hand quality;
- turn Greymond enters;
- whether Greymond entered with 4+ Humans;
- cards drawn by turn 7;
- cards left after the first board wipe;
- token count generated by turn 7;
- whether the deck could produce a lethal attack in 1–2 combat steps;
- matchups where taxes/protection were decisive;
- games lost because of mana or utility lands.
