## 1. Identity

**Commander:** Lynde, Cheerful Tormentor  
**Color Identity:** Grixis  
**Theme:** Curses  
**Subthemes:** Politics / Punisher / Pillow Fort / Enchantment Engine / Copy / Recursion

### Frase operacional

> Este é um deck Grixis Curses / Politics / Punisher que transforma Curses em recursos renováveis. Lynde recicla Curses destruídas, redistribui-as pela mesa para gerar card advantage e utiliza engines de encantamentos, cópias, sacrifícios e múltiplas upkeeps para transformar pequenas punições em uma inevitabilidade.

## 5. Main Strategic Engine

### Lynde

- Curse que sai do campo e vai ao graveyard → retorna no próximo end step anexada a você.
- No upkeep, pode mover uma Curse sua para um oponente.
- Se mover, compra 2 cartas.

### Sacrifice loop

**Curse → Claws of Gix / Despotic Scepter → graveyard → Lynde → volta → move → draw 2**

### Upkeep multiplier

**Paradox Haze → segunda upkeep → segunda oportunidade de trigger da Lynde**

## 6. Early Game

**Objetivo:** estabelecer mana, colocar uma Curse e preparar Lynde.

Prioridades:
- Sol Ring / Arcane Signet / Talismans
- Curse of Opulence e Curses baratas
- Entity Tracker / Accursed Witch
- Lynde no turno 4 sempre que possível

Não é um deck que precisa “ganhar cedo”. O early game serve para preparar inevitabilidade.

## 7. Mid Game

Prioridades:
- Bitterheart Witch
- Curse of Misfortunes
- Entity Tracker
- Paradox Haze
- cópias das melhores Curses
- Claws of Gix / Despotic Scepter

## 14. Rules Interactions to Preserve

### Lynde return

Official release notes confirm that a Curse put into your graveyard from the battlefield returns at the beginning of the next end step attached to you.

### Lynde upkeep

The movement ability does **not target**, so a player having hexproof does not prevent the Curse from being attached.

### Paradox Haze

An additional upkeep means upkeep-triggered abilities can trigger again in that additional upkeep.

### Curse subtype

Curses are Auras that enchant players and otherwise use normal Aura rules.

## 16. Decisions That Must Not Be Lost

- The deck is **Curses-first**.
- Do not optimize by turning it into Grixis Enchantress Goodstuff.
- Curses are the identity, not merely a subtheme.
- Political/chaotic cards have a higher tolerance here than in an optimized generic list.
- Claws of Gix and Despotic Scepter are strategic pieces, not random artifacts.
- Paradox Haze is a major engine.
- Bitterheart Witch is a thematic tutor.
- Entity Tracker is a key draw engine.
- Current and Projected must remain separate.
- 8,9 is **not** a tested score.
